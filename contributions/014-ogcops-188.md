## 1. Problem and requirements

The expanded platform preview in the editor needed a complete keyboard interaction, including focus management while open and after dismissal.

## 2. Implementation

Opening the preview moves focus into its dialog. Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside it; Escape and the Close control dismiss it and return focus to the opening card. The dialog is labelled with the platform name, and backdrop dismissal remains available.

The maintainer confirmed keyboard and mouse behavior in headless Chrome; current `dev` had 657 passing tests and a successful build.

## 4. Maintainer review

## 5. What I learned

- Accessible dialogs need focus entry, containment, a reliable exit, and focus restoration. Test both keyboard and pointer dismissal paths.
